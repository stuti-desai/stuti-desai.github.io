Why regulatory affairs

My dad has worked in this industry for more than 40 years.

During COVID, the world rightly thanked doctors and scientists for showing up every day. I thought about my dad too, going in every day to make sure medicines actually got made and actually got approved.

Nobody clapped for that. I noticed anyway.

That's where this started for me.
